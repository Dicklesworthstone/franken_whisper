=== CONCLUSIONS (admissible on thinkstation1, T=32) ===

1. THE PARKED WORRY IS FALSIFIED ON THIS BOX. The 5975WX baseline had naive-4x8 REGRESS fc1
   (0.958x); on thinkstation1 naive-4x8 gives fc1 1.143x — a clean WIN. fc1 4x8 was host-
   specific. Here the naive uniform 4x8 grid is a clean bit-exact win on ALL THREE shapes
   (qkv 1.008x ~neutral, fc1 1.143x, fc2 1.126x) => LAYER 1.089x => projected e2e ~1.056x.
   This is the win the lever was parked waiting to size. It is ALREADY IMPLEMENTED and gated
   behind set_sgemm_tile_balanced() (frankentorch 86a54f1a), default OFF.

2. THE B-BYTES-AWARE SELECTOR ADDS ~NOTHING HERE. The refined rule (largest divisor q<=T/2 with
   ceil(n/q)>=MIN_BLOCK_COLS; p>=2 avoids the p=1 A-locality cliff) DOES pick the per-shape
   optimum (qkv/fc2 4x8, fc1 2x16), but the per-shape optimum is only 1.098x vs naive's 1.089x
   — a ~0.8% layer delta driven entirely by fc1 (2x16 1.176x vs 4x8 1.143x), and that 2.9% fc1
   gap is within ~1 cv (4%) of the noise. NOT worth the API/complexity of a shape-dependent
   policy on a shared, shipped crate. The naive uniform grid captures the win.

3. RECOMMENDATION (owner-gated, cross-repo shared crate — surface, do not flip unilaterally):
   flip set_sgemm_tile_balanced default to ON. It is bit-exact (certified host-independent),
   the fc1-regression concern that blocked it does not reproduce on the target box, and it is a
   clean +1.089x on the turbo linear-GEMM layer (~1.056x e2e). Residual risk is only the FMA/
   golden-f32 shared-crate concern noted in project_isa_baseline, unrelated to this grid choice.
