# FrankenWhisper Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 14, 2026

FrankenWhisper is designed to transcribe and label speech locally on your device. The
app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking, and the FrankenWhisper
developer does not collect personal data from the app.

## Data processed on your device

- Microphone recordings you make in the app are processed only on your device.
- Audio files you choose from Files are read and processed only on your device.
- Transcripts, word timestamps, detected speaker lanes, speaker names you enter, and
  denoised audio processing remain on your device.
- Exported HTML, Markdown, text, SRT, and JSON files leave the app only when you
  explicitly use the system share sheet.
- Downloaded model files are stored in the app's private storage and excluded from
  device backups. You can clear the downloaded models from within the app.

FrankenWhisper does not upload recordings, imported audio, transcripts, speaker labels,
speaker names, or exports.

## Network access

With your consent, the app downloads approximately 1.4 GB of speech-recognition,
speaker-labeling, and denoising model files from the FrankenWhisper project's Hugging
Face repository or GitHub Releases. These requests do not contain your audio,
transcripts, or speaker information. The hosting provider may receive routine network
request information, such as an IP address and user agent, under its own privacy policy.
The FrankenWhisper developer does not receive this request information from the app.

## Permissions

- Microphone access is requested only when you choose to record audio. You can deny or
  revoke this permission in iOS Settings and continue importing audio files.
- Importing audio uses Apple's system document picker, which gives FrankenWhisper
  access only to the file you select.

## Children

Because FrankenWhisper does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect
personal data from children.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this document with a new effective date. For
privacy questions, open an issue in the
[FrankenWhisper repository](https://github.com/Dicklesworthstone/franken_whisper/issues).
